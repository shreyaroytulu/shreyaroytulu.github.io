Undergraduate research

Environmental Impact Assessment and Sustainability Pathways of Chlor-Alkali Industries in Bangladesh

Supervised by Md. Delowar Hossain, Assistant Professor, Dept. of CEP, SUST

  • Collected operational data during factory visits to Global Heavy Chemicals Ltd. (GHCL): brine concentrations, NaOH production rates and power consumption.
  • Performed a material and energy balance for a 70 ton/day NaOH membrane cell plant using field-measured data.
  • Estimated that 211.6 tons/month of untreated HCl was being released into the Buriganga River.
  • Proposed mitigation pathways: effluent treatment design, hydrogen use for process heating, and brine sludge reuse as construction material.
  • Presented the findings as a poster at the Department of Chemical Engineering and Polymer Science, SUST.

Awards and recognition

September 2026

9th Japan Construction International Award, Pioneering Activity Category

I’m proud to share that JDC Corporation’s JaPani project, which I work on as a chemical engineer, received the 9th Japan Construction International Award in the Pioneering Activity Category.

The award recognizes our validation survey for a decentralized village water supply in rural Bangladesh, where arsenic in groundwater is a serious problem. The project uses the JaPani system to provide safe and reliable drinking water to rural communities.

On the team, I lead field operations and performance monitoring at our pilot arsenic removal plants and track water quality against the WHO guideline of 10 μg/L. This is a team achievement, and I’m grateful to be part of it.
